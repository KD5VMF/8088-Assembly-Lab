Galaxy16 quick start
====================

1. Build:
   TASM Galaxy16.asm
   TLINK /T Galaxy16.OBJ

2. Run:
   Galaxy16

3. Keys while running:
   F      Force 8087 floating-point engine, if detected
   I      Force 8088 integer fallback engine
   Q/ESC  Quit

Notes:
- This program does not change video mode.
- It assumes an 80-column color text mode using B800h video RAM.
- It does not write to disk while running.
- The on-screen title remains GALAXY MATH 88 REV16.
